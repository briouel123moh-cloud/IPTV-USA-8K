# IPTV USA 8K content adaptation

Reviewed 1 November 2026. This release uses the Canada site's interaction and editorial structure with a US-specific English content set. Legacy `fr` data keys are English fallbacks for shared component compatibility; they do not create French pages. The legacy guide identifier `french-tv-usa` now identifies the Spanish-viewing guide at `/spanish-iptv-usa/`.

## Coverage

- 21 distinct search title, description and H1 sets; all titles and descriptions unique, no title over 60 characters or description over 160.
- Ten homepage topic sections, each with three substantive subtopics.
- Dedicated US channels, sports, and movies/series pages with practical tables and FAQs.
- Eight guides: subscription cost, setup, buffering, multi-device households, choosing a provider, trial testing, IPTV versus cable, and Spanish/bilingual viewing.
- 36 questions in six task-based FAQ categories.
- Device chooser that distinguishes Fire OS and Vega OS instead of making a blanket Firestick compatibility claim.

Purchase intent leads the page map: subscription plans and USD quotes, trial access, simultaneous connections, device compatibility, local channels and Spanish-language viewing. No measured keyword volume, difficulty, CPC or ranking position is invented.

## US-specific distinctions

The content asks for ZIP code and exact affiliate when checking ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX or PBS. Spanish viewing covers exact Univision/Telemundo feeds, audio versus subtitles, accented search and simultaneous English/Spanish use. Sports examples use NFL, NBA, MLB and college events as interests to verify, not included-rights promises. Pricing remains a real USD quote for the selected duration/screens. The seven-day program remains daily renewal, one request after 24 hours, up to seven sessions in 30 days; each session has its own expiry.

Channel, league and promotional-title references are illustrative. No catalog counts, ratings, licenses, native 8K streaming, guaranteed speeds or published service tariffs were invented. The hypothetical cost calculation is explicitly labeled as an arithmetic example, not an IPTV USA 8K price.
