# IPTV market expansion research — 1 November 2026

## Decision

After the USA and Canada, **Australia is the strongest next English-language test**. **Germany** is the strongest researched option for a larger, authorized-provider comparison/affiliate business; **Switzerland** is a smaller multilingual premium-market test. Treat **New Zealand** as an extension of an Australian operation, and **Norway** as a later localized experiment.

This is a judgment based on adoption, existing commercial offers, language cost and available business models. It is **not** a measured keyword-volume, CPC, keyword-difficulty or profitability ranking. No paid keyword dataset was available. Broad streaming adoption is evidence of audience behaviour, not proof of search volume for “IPTV”. Search observations are a current sample, not a stable Google rank report.

## Are people actually selling IPTV there?

The sampled search results include active localized offer pages in Australia, Switzerland and Norway: iptvsaustralia.com/pricing, iptvvsuisse.com/preise and sveastream.tv/no/priser. They advertise local-currency plans and trial/device options. This establishes **visible supply and direct-response intent only**. Their revenue, uptime, customer satisfaction, claimed channel counts and content authorization were not independently verified. Do not reproduce those claims or use them to infer low competition.

Licensed/established offers also demonstrate competition and customer price anchors: Fetch/Kayo in Australia, Zattoo in Switzerland, waipu.tv in Germany and Sky Sport Now in New Zealand. These packages are not interchangeable; prices cannot be compared as if the same channels, sport rights, hardware and contract terms were included.

## Why a country's sites “drop”

First distinguish ranking loss from deindexing, hosting/domain suspension, copyright removals and ISP blocking. Google's own documentation identifies access/indexing failures, competitive/content changes and algorithm changes as causes of traffic drops. Manual actions can lower or omit a site from results. Without a site's Search Console data and suspension notice, its disappearance does not establish the cause.

It is not supported to say the USA, Canada, Australia, Switzerland or the Nordics are safe alternatives for unauthorized streaming. In June 2026, the US DOJ announced seizure of nearly 400 domains offering unauthorized World Cup streams. A February 2026 Eurojust operation included authorities from Canada alongside several European and other countries. Australia's existing scheme can target overseas sites and search-engine links. Sweden's government has published an investigation proposing stronger measures against illegal IPTV; this source is a proposal record, not proof of a newly enacted consumer ban.

The durable business advantage is authorized distribution/approved affiliates, useful local support, honest terms, original experience-based content and actual customer retention. Geography alone cannot secure a website's survival. No enforcement-evasion or domain-hopping strategy is recommended.

## Practical expansion sequence

1. Finish the USA site and measure the existing Canada/US funnels before opening several more countries.
2. Validate Australia with a compact set of genuinely distinct pages covering subscriptions, trial terms, devices and multi-screen needs. Record trial-to-sale rate, retention and net contribution; published subscriptions are not profit.
3. Explore an approved German affiliate relationship in parallel if native-language capacity is available. Do not describe an application as approval.
4. Expand into Switzerland only with German/French localization and a concrete differentiated offer; add New Zealand through shared operations after Australia produces evidence.
5. Obtain actual country-level search volumes and SERP histories before choosing domains based on alleged easy keywords. The candidate terms above are hypotheses grounded in intent, not numeric keyword research.
